Welcome

Our goals and missions are based on God's Word, and our main focus is leading our community to Christ. Whoever you are, you are welcome here.

Faith Baptist Church was founded in 1989 by Pastor Brian Mitchell and his family. In 1996 the church bought its own building at 190 Center Street, with a parsonage on the same property, where we still meet today.

Our pastor

CT

Dr. Chris Tefft

Pastor since 2015, with his wife Brenda

Pastor Tefft grew up in Rhode Island and served in the 82nd Airborne. After Bible college he taught at a Christian school and then as a Bible professor at his alma mater, before accepting the call to Faith Baptist. He and Brenda want to help each person grow in Bible knowledge and in a personal walk with God.

What we believe

Draft wording for Pastor Tefft to approve
The Scriptures

We believe the Holy Bible is the inspired, preserved Word of God, without error, and our final authority. We use the King James Version.

2 Timothy 3:16–17; Psalm 12:6–7

God

We believe in one God, eternally existing in three persons: the Father, the Son and the Holy Ghost.

1 John 5:7; Matthew 28:19

The Lord Jesus Christ

We believe in His virgin birth, sinless life, death on the cross for our sins, bodily resurrection, and His coming again.

Isaiah 7:14; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

Salvation

We believe salvation is a free gift, by grace through faith in Christ alone, not of works, and that one who is truly saved is kept forever.

Ephesians 2:8–9; John 10:28–29

The Church

We believe in the independent, self-governing local church, with Christ as its head, and in two ordinances: baptism by immersion of believers, and the Lord's Supper.

Matthew 16:18; Acts 2:41–42

Things to come

We believe in the personal, bodily return of the Lord Jesus Christ for His church.

1 Thessalonians 4:16–17; John 14:3
