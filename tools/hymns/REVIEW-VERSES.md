# Extra verses for the hymns — for review

Drafted for each hymn: the verses its words **quote or clearly echo**. 1035 verses for 222 of the 224 hymns. Every verse below was checked against the app's own KJV text, every quoted line is really in the hymn, and none repeats a verse the hymn already lists.

**Reviewed 12 October 2026: all kept.** (Psalm 145:10 on Holy, Holy, Holy and John 20:20 on Crown Him with Many Crowns were struck at first, then kept as close echoes.)

**How to review.** Each verse shows the hymn line that echoes it and the KJV words.
- **Ticked** `[x]` (849): the hymn and the verse share words. They go in unless you untick them.
- **Unticked** `[ ]` (186): an echo of an image or thought rather than the words (marked *image*). Tick the ones you want.

Then `node tools/hymns/apply-review.js` adds the ticked verses to `tools/hymns/list.js` and rebuilds the hymns (or ask Claude to). `--dry-run` shows what it would add first.

Points the drafters raised for a pastor's eye: *Rock of Ages* → Isaiah 26:4 (the KJV reads "everlasting strength"; "rock of ages" is the margin); *And Can It Be* → Philippians 2:7 ("emptied" follows the Greek, not the KJV's "made himself of no reputation"); *Dare to Be a Daniel* → Daniel 3:18 (spoken by the three friends); *Beulah Land* → Revelation 22 (the hymn's Beulah is a foretaste, not heaven itself).
