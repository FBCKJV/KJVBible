🔊 Audio Bible — Two Ways to Listen
📖Book Play — From any book's chapter list, tap the 🔊 Listen button below the chapters to stream the entire book. A floating player bar stays with you across all screens — read along, search, or navigate while the audio continues. Your position in every book is saved automatically.
🔊Chapter Play — While reading, tap the 🔊 button next to the chapter title to hear just that chapter. Audio plays to the end of the last verse, then stops automatically. Pause or leave partway and tapping 🔊 again picks up right where you left off; once a chapter finishes it starts fresh next time.
▶Both modes share the same floating player bar with play/pause, ◀30 / 30▶ skip, speed control (0.5×–2.0×), and a progress bar. Chapter Play shows chapter progress; Book Play shows whole-book progress.
💾Both Book Play and Chapter Play positions are saved to the cloud with your restore code — switch devices and pick up exactly where you left off, whether in a whole book or a single chapter.
⚠️ Important
☁️Your streaks, badges, bookmarks, highlights, notes and progress are automatically backed up to the cloud using your restore code. You can safely switch devices or browsers at any time. Avoid clearing site data for fbckjv.app in Chrome settings as this removes your local copy — your cloud backup will still be intact via your restore code.
