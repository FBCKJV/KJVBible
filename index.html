💡 Tips & Shortcuts
⚙The ⚙ gear (top-right) holds all your settings — text size, day / night / sepia theme, reading font, line spacing, audio options, offline download, and your backup code — and, at the top, these tips and 📤 Share this app (a QR code to hand the app to someone)
📝The 📝 notebook (top bar) keeps your sermon and study notes. Tap 📝＋ on any verse, search result, hymn, word, person, place, map or timeline event to add it to the study you have open; select several verses to add a whole passage. Headings, outlines, quote boxes, bold and italic; share as text, copy it as Markdown (for Claude, Google Docs or a slide maker), or print to PDF from ☰ Studies.
📖Tap a verse to open its card — it lists any archaic words (with KJV dictionary definitions) and the people & places in that verse with their name meanings
✦Gold ✦ markers next to verse numbers link Old Testament quotations both ways — in the New Testament they show the OT source; in the Old Testament they show where the NT quotes it
☐Long press any verse to enter select mode — then copy, share, highlight, bookmark, or create a screenshot card
📸Screenshot cards come in 5 colour themes and both landscape and portrait (TikTok/Stories) formats
🔥The flame badge in the header tracks your daily reading streak — tap it to see your progress and milestone badges
🔍Search understands references — type John 3:16, Matt 24, or 1 Pet 3 to jump directly to that passage
❝Search for an exact phrase — put it in quotes, like "god so loved". Put a minus before a word to leave it out: love -god. Switch between Best match and Bible order above the results
⇄Parallel passage chips appear automatically when reading a Gospel chapter that has a counterpart in another Gospel
📑Search → Doctrines has Faith Baptist Church's positions on salvation, the Godhead, baptism, last things and more — each with supporting KJV verses
📚Search → Topics has Nave's Topical Bible — ~5,300 subjects A–Z, each with its passages arranged by sub-topic. Searching a subject like forgiveness shows its topic card at the top of your results
📖Search → Dictionary is Webster's 1828 dictionary with our church's KJV notes on top — type any word, or browse the KJV word list. Its Scripture references are links
📇Search → Concordance browses every KJV word from A–Z with its verse count — tap a word to find every verse it appears in
📍Search → Names also lists ~1,200 Bible places — tap one to see where it was today, how far from Jerusalem, what was nearby, and every verse that names it
👤Search → Names lists ~2,600 names with their meanings and the ~3,000 people who bore them — tap a name to see who each person was, their family, and every verse that names them. Tap a verse while reading to see the people in it
🗺️Search → Maps shows every Bible place on one map — tap a place to see what happened there. The classic atlas maps are below it. All of it works offline. In the reader, 📍 Where maps the places in the chapter
📅Plans tab — run Bible in a Year, NT in 30 Days and Proverbs in a Month, one or several at once, each at your own pace. A chapter checks off once you have read to the end of it; ✓ Mark read is for a day read from a printed Bible. Your next reading of each plan is on Home; ⋯ pauses, starts over or removes a plan
📝Notes tab — everything you have marked or written: Bookmarks, Highlights, verse Notes and your Studies from the 📝 notebook (tap one to open it, or ＋ New study). The search box looks through all of them
🏠Home — a card for each bottom tab under the books: Search & Study Tools (type in the box, or open the arrow for every tool), Reading Plans and Notes. An arrow that turns folds a card open; › goes to its page. Home remembers which cards you left open
📚Reading Journey (bottom of Plans) — track and share which of the 66 books you've read
⚔️Sword Drill (home screen) hides verses in your heart with fill-in-the-blank memory, spaced review and badges — add any verse you like under My Verses

🔊 Audio Bible — Two Ways to Listen
📖Book Play — From any book's chapter list, tap the 🔊 Listen button below the chapters to stream the entire book. A floating player bar stays with you across all screens — read along, search, or navigate while the audio continues. Your position in every book is saved automatically.
🔊Chapter Play — While reading, tap the 🔊 button next to the chapter title to hear just that chapter. Audio plays to the end of the last verse, then stops automatically. Pause or leave partway and tapping 🔊 again picks up right where you left off; once a chapter finishes it starts fresh next time.
🌙Sleep timer & Auto-play — Tap the 🌙 on the player bar to wind down after 15, 30, or 60 minutes. Turn on Auto-play next chapter in ⚙ Settings to keep listening — and if you're reading the chapter that's playing, the text follows along as it advances.
▶Both modes share the same floating player bar with play/pause, ◀30 / 30▶ skip, speed control (0.5×–2.0×), and a progress bar. Chapter Play shows chapter progress; Book Play shows whole-book progress.
💾Both Book Play and Chapter Play positions are saved to the cloud with your restore code — switch devices and pick up exactly where you left off, whether in a whole book or a single chapter.
⚠️ Important
☁️Your streaks, badges, bookmarks, highlights, notes and progress are automatically backed up to the cloud using your restore code. You can safely switch devices or browsers at any time. Avoid clearing site data for fbckjv.app in Chrome settings as this removes your local copy — your cloud backup will still be intact via your restore code.

What's New
What's new in this update
🏠
A Tidier Home & a Notes Tab
Home is now one set of matching cards. Under the books are a card for Search & Study Tools (search right from Home, or open it for every tool), your Reading Plans and your Notes, then Sword Drill. The Saved tab is now Notes: bookmarks, highlights, verse notes and now your Studies, all in one place.
📅
Reading Plans, Your Way
Run more than one plan at once — say Proverbs in a Month alongside the New Testament in 30 Days — each at your own pace, with your next reading of each right on Home. A chapter now counts once you have read to the end of it (not just by opening it), and the end of each chapter shows what is next. Pause a plan or start it over without losing the others; finished plans are kept with their dates. Your bookmarks, highlights, notes and studies now sync between devices without one overwriting another.
🎞️
Teach From Your Study
Write your lesson in the 📝 Study Notebook, then tap 🎦 Present — it becomes slides: a title slide, a slide for each heading and its points, and every verse large on its own. ✂ starts a new slide wherever you like, and a lesson pasted in keeps its headings and lists. While presenting, 🔎 (or the / key) jumps to any verse and ↩ Lesson brings you back. Journeys can be presented too: the map full screen, the stop and its verse in large type.
🗂️
Two-Column Search on Big Screens
On a tablet, computer or classroom panel, Topics, Names, the Dictionary and Hymns keep their list on the left and open each page on the right. Search results, the concordance, the timeline, journeys and your saved verses spread across the screen, and the map shows a place’s story beside it. The Reading Plans cards look right again, too.
📽
Classroom Present & Resizable Panels
Present mode adds 📽 Classroom — one verse, never smaller than a back row can read, with long verses running onto a second screen — two high-contrast themes for projectors, and 🖱 Clicker only to hide the buttons. Your Present settings now travel with your restore code. On a tablet or computer, drag the edge of a side panel to resize it; double-tap the edge to reset.
🖥️
Made for Tablets and Big Screens
On an iPad, a laptop or a classroom panel, the verse card, the map and the Study Notebook now open beside the chapter instead of over it — read on the left, study on the right. Journeys show the stop card beside the map, and buttons are larger for a finger at the board.
🎦
Present Mode
Put Scripture on the big screen — a projector, TV or classroom panel. Tap 🎦 Present under a chapter or in the verse card: one to three verses at a time, as large as the screen allows. Step on with the arrows, a tap or swipe, the keyboard or a presentation clicker; switch to a light page for bright rooms.
🧭
Bible Journeys
Follow eighteen journeys stop by stop — Abraham, the Exodus, David, Elijah, Jonah, the captivity, the Lord Jesus, and Paul all the way to Rome. Each stop is a verse that names the place, with its date on the timeline; tap Next stop › and watch the route draw on. Find them in Search → Maps, under a chapter (🧭), and on the timeline, where every event now has 📍 for its places.
🗺️
What Happened Here?
Search → Maps now opens a map with every place in the Bible. Tap a town, or find one by name, to see what happened there — its chapters grouped from the patriarchs to the early church, each opening to the verses. The classic atlas maps are just below, and in the reader they’re a link inside 📍 Where.
📍
Where Is This Taking Place?
A parchment map of the Bible lands, drawn right in the app and working offline. Tap 📍 Where under a chapter title to see every place in the chapter pinned — the Bible name with today’s name. Pinch or scroll to zoom from the whole region down to the towns nearby, and drag to look around. The map is also in the verse card (🗺️ Show on map) and on every place page.
⚔️
Sword Drill, Rebuilt
The verse memory game has four ways to practice — 📖 Learn, 🔤 First Letters, 🧩 Word Order and ⚔ Full Recall. Type in one bar above the keyboard — each word is accepted the moment it’s right, no Enter. Use a 💡 hint when you’re stuck and earn up to three stars. Practice next picks your next verse.
✦
Clearer Cross-References
Tap a verse and its cross-references are listed plainly — where the New Testament quotes it (✦), and the key words with their references. Tap any reference to read it right there; tap a word or See all for the full list. The gold ✦ beside a verse number is easier to tap and opens the quotation at once.
🧭
A New Search Page
Every study tool is now laid out on the Search page — Doctrines, Topics, Dictionary, Concordance, Names, Timeline, Maps and Hymns. Back walks you back step by step to Search, then Home, and ↩ Continue where you were picks up right where you left off.
📝
Sermon & Study Notebook
Tap 📝 in the top bar to write a study — headings, numbered outlines, quote boxes, bold and italic. Then build it as you go: tap 📝＋ on any verse, search result, hymn, dictionary word, person, place, map or timeline event and it’s added with its reference. Select several verses to add a whole passage.
🖨️
Share or Print Your Study
Keep as many studies as you like under ☰ Studies. Share one as text, copy it, or print it — or save it as a PDF — for the pulpit or a class handout. Tap any reference in a study to read it in context. Studies are saved with your backup.
🎵
The Hymnal
Search → Hymns holds 98 beloved hymns — Amazing Grace, Blessed Assurance, It Is Well, The Old Rugged Cross and more — with every stanza, the refrain, and the Scriptures behind each hymn. Find one by title, author, or any line you remember.
⭐
First Mention
In the Concordance and Dictionary, the first verse a word appears in is marked — grace first appears in Genesis 6:8.
🔗
Follow the Thread
On a Compare Scripture page, tap 🔗 Compare on any verse to open its own references — and follow a truth from verse to verse.
🔗
Compare Scripture
Tap any verse while reading and its key phrases appear under Compare Scripture — from the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge, 300,000 cross-references for nearly every verse. Tap a phrase like “the beginning” to read the Scriptures that shed light on it.
📅
Bible Timeline
Search → Timeline walks from the Creation to the Revelation in 96 events, each with its verse, dated by Ussher’s chronology from the old KJV margins. While reading, a 📅 date button shows when the chapter took place.
📜
Concordance Verses
Tap a word in Search → Concordance and its verses open right there, grouped by book with the word marked — no more jumping over to Search.
📍
Bible Places
Search → Names now includes 1,200 places. Tap one to see where it was — the modern site, how sure scholars are, its distance from Jerusalem, nearby Bible places, a map link, and every verse that names it. Tap a verse while reading to see the places in it.
📖
Webster’s 1828 Dictionary
Search → Dictionary now holds Noah Webster’s 1828 dictionary — 62,000 words defined as they were understood near the King James era, with tappable Scripture references. Our church’s KJV notes sit right on top of each entry — now including baptism, the elect and predestination. Tap a verse while reading to see its hard words, then open the full entry.
👤
Bible People
Search → Names now knows the 3,000 people of the Bible: tap a name to see who they were, what their name means in Hebrew or Greek, their family, and every verse that names them. Same-named people — like the 24 Zechariahs — are told apart.
🔗
Everything Connected
Tap a verse to see the people in it and the doctrines that cite it. Chapters with a Bible map show a 🗺️ map button. Your recent searches wait under the search box, and Concordance words can include their other forms (reign, reigned, reigneth).
📚
Bible Topics
Search a subject like forgiveness, prayer or what does the Bible say about anger and a topic card opens every passage on it, arranged by sub-topic — from Nave’s Topical Bible. Or browse all 5,300 topics A–Z under Search → Topics.
✏️
Did You Mean…?
Misspell a word — beleive, Nebuchadnezer, rightousness — and search offers the right KJV spelling with one tap.
🔍
Smarter Search
Search finds the verse you mean: type a phrase like be still and know and the best match comes first, or tap Bible order to read results Genesis to Revelation. Words match whole words (sin finds sinned and sinful, not “business”). Put words in quotes for an exact phrase, or a minus to leave one out: love -god.
📖
More Ways to Type a Reference
Search understands Rev. 22:21, 1John 3:16, First John 4:8, Jude 5, and ranges across chapters like John 3:16-4:2.
⚡
Faster & Works Offline
Books are now saved on your device as you use the app, so search is quicker and Scripture opens even without a connection. Download All 66 Books in Settings now saves every book. Back from a doctrine verse now returns you to that doctrine.
